# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the **Forge CLI** project pledge to
make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

We are building a **developer infrastructure tool** — a project that will be
used by engineers to create and maintain software foundations. The quality of
our community directly affects the quality of our product. We take this
responsibility seriously.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

### ✅ Positive Behaviors

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people.
- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback.
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our
  mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the
  overall community and the users of Forge CLI.
- **Being patient** with newcomers and those learning the project.
- **Explaining your reasoning** when you disagree, rather than simply
  dismissing another person's view.
- **Crediting others' work** — if you build on someone else's idea, say so.
- **Asking clarifying questions** before assuming bad intent.

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **The use of sexualized language or imagery**, and sexual attention or
  advances of any kind.
- **Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments**, and personal or political
  attacks.
- **Public or private harassment** of any kind.
- **Publishing others' private information**, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- **Dismissing or belittling** someone's contribution because of their
  experience level, background, or identity.
- **Sustained disruption** of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- **Spamming** issues, pull requests, or discussions with unrelated content.
- **Other conduct** which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

### Community Leaders

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:

- **Remove, edit, or reject** comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and
  other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
- **Communicate reasons for moderation decisions** when appropriate.
- **Temporarily or permanently ban** any contributor for other behaviors that
  they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- **GitHub repository** (issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki, code reviews).
- **GitHub Discussions** and other project forums.
- **Community chat** (e.g., Discord, Slack, if applicable).
- **Mailing lists** and email communications.
- **Social media** when representing the project.
- **In-person events** (meetups, conferences) when representing the project.

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is **officially
representing the project** in public spaces. Examples of representing our
project include using an official email address, posting via an official social
media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline
event.

## Reporting

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior — or have any other
concerns — please report it as soon as possible to the project maintainers at:

**📧 [conduct@forge-cli.dev](mailto:conduct@forge-cli.dev)**

All reports will be handled with discretion. Please include:

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Your contact info** | So we can follow up (optional — anonymous reports are accepted). |
| **Names of those involved** | Who was involved in the incident. |
| **Description of the incident** | What happened, and when. |
| **Context** | Links, screenshots, or logs (if applicable). |
| **Impact** | How the incident affected you or others. |
| **Any prior incidents** | If this is part of a pattern. |

### What Happens Next

| Timeline | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Within 48 hours** | We acknowledge receipt of your report. |
| **Within 5 business days** | We provide an initial assessment and a proposed response. |
| **Ongoing** | We keep you informed of progress, to the extent we can while respecting privacy. |
| **On resolution** | We communicate the outcome and any actions taken. |

### Confidentiality

All reports will be kept confidential. We will not share your identity with the
person involved unless you explicitly ask us to, or unless it is necessary to
protect you or others.

### Retaliation

**Retaliation against anyone who reports an incident in good faith is
prohibited.** We will treat retaliation as a separate violation of this Code of
Conduct and will respond accordingly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these **Community Impact Guidelines** in
determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code
of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels
like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent
ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or
private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction
with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
community.

## Forge-Specific Additions

### Developer Tooling and Inclusivity

Forge CLI is a tool for **all software developers**. Our community must reflect
the diversity of the people who write software. We are committed to:

- **Welcoming newcomers** to the project and to software engineering more
  broadly.
- **Using inclusive language** in code, documentation, and discussions (e.g.,
  "allowlist" / "denylist" instead of "whitelist" / "blacklist").
- **Documenting decisions** so that people from all backgrounds can
  participate.
- **Providing clear contribution pathways** for people with different levels of
  experience.
- **Mentoring** contributors who are new to open source.

### Technical Disagreements

Disagreements about technical decisions are normal and healthy. We ask that:

- **Critique ideas, not people.** "This approach has a problem because…" is
  acceptable; "You don't understand how this works" is not.
- **Provide evidence.** If you disagree with a design decision, explain *why*
  with examples, benchmarks, or references.
- **Assume good intent.** If a comment could be read charitably or
  uncharitably, read it charitably.
- **Escalate respectfully.** If a disagreement cannot be resolved, ask a
  maintainer to mediate.

### Reporting Maintainers

If you have a concern about the behavior of a **maintainer**, please report it
to `conduct@forge-cli.dev`. If the concern involves the person monitoring that
address, please contact a different maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`.
